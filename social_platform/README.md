# Nexus Social Media Platform

A modular full-stack social media backend supporting:
- **Feed & Posts**: Public posts, friends-only posts, and private drafts with reactions and comments.
- **Granular Privacy Engine**: Visibility levels (`PUBLIC`, `FRIENDS_ONLY`, `RESTRICTED`, `PRIVATE`) and blocklist enforcement.
- **Real-Time Chat**: Direct channels, group messaging, and connection management.
- **WebRTC Calls**: Audio and video call signaling, SDP offer/answer negotiation, and ICE routing.
- **Dual Storage**: Public CDN for public media, Encrypted Vault for private items and DMs.
